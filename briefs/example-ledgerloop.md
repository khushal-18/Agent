# LedgerLoop (fictional example brief)

We are building LedgerLoop, an AI-powered, next-generation inventory intelligence platform that
revolutionises how direct-to-consumer brands manage stock. Our proprietary machine-learning engine
forecasts demand and tells brands exactly how much to reorder and when.

Founders: two ex-operations managers from a mid-size fashion D2C brand. Team of 4. No funding yet.
Product: a Shopify plugin plus a dashboard. Connects to Shopify orders, shows forecasts per SKU,
sends reorder alerts by email. Pricing idea: $199/month.
Traction: 3 friendly brands using a prototype for 6 weeks. Founders say "they love it".
We think our target market is every online retailer, which is a huge opportunity.
Goal: first 50 paying customers in 6 months.
